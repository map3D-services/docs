---
title: "Analyse > Options > Arrêter en cas d'erreur"
---

{/* i18n:untranslated */}
<Note>Cette page n'a pas encore été traduite en français. Le contenu est affiché en anglais.</Note>

When checked, if errors are detected during intersection, discretization or BEM analysis, the analysis is halted.

At this point you can force the analysis to proceed with known errors if you so choose.

When not checked, the analysis proceeds even when errors are detected.
